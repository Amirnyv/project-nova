FAQ

Questions about Nova.

What is Project Nova?

Nova is an AI-powered workspace that combines AI conversations, projects, notes, tasks and files in one place.

Is Nova only for students?

No. Nova is designed for anyone who wants one organized place for projects, everyday work and AI assistance. Student-focused tools are an additional direction we're developing.

Does Student Offline Mode work now?

Not yet. Student Offline Mode is an upcoming feature and is currently in development.

Do I need an account?

You can explore this website without an account. An account is required to use the Nova workspace.
